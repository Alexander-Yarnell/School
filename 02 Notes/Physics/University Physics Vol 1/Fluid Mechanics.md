# Fluid Mechanics

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 1]] › Unit 1 — Mechanics › Chapter 14
> ← [[Gravitation]] · Next: [[Oscillations]] →

**Fluid Mechanics** applies mechanics to liquids and gases — first at rest (pressure, buoyancy), then in motion (continuity, Bernoulli, viscosity).

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> - **Density:** $\displaystyle \rho = \frac{m}{V}$
> - **Pressure:** $\displaystyle p = \frac{F}{A}$
> - **Pressure at depth:** $\displaystyle p = p_0 + \rho g h$
> - **Pressure change with height:** $\displaystyle \frac{dp}{dy} = -\rho g$
> - **Absolute pressure:** $\displaystyle p_{\text{abs}} = p_g + p_{\text{atm}}$
> - **Pascal's principle:** $\displaystyle \frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2}$
> - **Volume flow rate:** $\displaystyle Q = \frac{dV}{dt}$
> - **Continuity equation:** $\displaystyle A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$
> - **Bernoulli's equation:** $\displaystyle p + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho g y = \text{constant}$
> - **Viscosity:** $\displaystyle \eta = \frac{FL}{vA}$
> - **Poiseuille's law:** $\displaystyle Q = \frac{(p_2 - p_1)\pi r^4}{8\eta l}$

## Fluids, Density, and Pressure
- Fluids (liquids and gases) flow under sideways or shearing forces.
- **Density** is mass per volume (kg/m³); **pressure** is force per perpendicular area (Pa = N/m²).
- Pressure in a liquid increases with depth.

> [!equation]
> - **Density:** $\displaystyle \rho = \frac{m}{V}$
> - **Pressure:** $\displaystyle p = \frac{F}{A}$
> - **Pressure at depth:** $\displaystyle p = p_0 + \rho g h$
> - **Pressure change with height:** $\displaystyle \frac{dp}{dy} = -\rho g$

## Measuring Pressure
- **Gauge pressure** is relative to atmospheric pressure; **absolute pressure** = gauge + atmospheric.
- Manometers measure pressure; mercury barometers measure atmospheric pressure.

> [!equation]
> - **Absolute pressure:** $\displaystyle p_{\text{abs}} = p_g + p_{\text{atm}}$

## Pascal's Principle and Hydraulics
- A pressure change in an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished everywhere — the basis of **hydraulic** systems.

> [!equation]
> - **Pascal's principle:** $\displaystyle \frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2}$

## Archimedes' Principle and Buoyancy
- The **buoyant force** equals the weight of the fluid displaced.
- If it exceeds the weight the object floats; if less it sinks; if equal it stays suspended.

## Fluid Dynamics
- **Flow rate** is volume per unit time and equals area times average velocity.
- The **continuity equation**: for an incompressible fluid, mass in = mass out.

> [!equation]
> - **Volume flow rate:** $\displaystyle Q = \frac{dV}{dt}$
> - **Continuity equation:** $\displaystyle A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$

## Bernoulli's Equation
- For an incompressible, frictionless fluid, pressure + KE per volume + gravitational PE per volume is constant along the flow.
- Applications include entrainment and velocity measurement.

> [!equation]
> - **Bernoulli's equation:** $\displaystyle p + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho g y = \text{constant}$

> [!note] Connection
> It is energy conservation per unit volume — compare [[Potential Energy and Conservation of Energy]].

## Viscosity and Turbulence
- **Laminar** flow is smooth layers; **turbulent** flow has eddies.
- **Viscosity** $\eta$ is internal fluid friction. Pressure drop = resistance × flow rate.
- The Reynolds number $N_R = 2\rho vr/\eta$: laminar below ~2000, turbulent above ~3000.

> [!equation]
> - **Viscosity:** $\displaystyle \eta = \frac{FL}{vA}$
> - **Poiseuille's law:** $\displaystyle Q = \frac{(p_2 - p_1)\pi r^4}{8\eta l}$

> [!note] Connection
> Viscosity appears in Stokes' drag law — [[Applications of Newton's Laws]].
