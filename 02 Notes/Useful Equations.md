### Basic Operations
#### Distributive Property
$$
	a(b + c) = ab + ac
$$
#### Commutative Property of Addition
$$
	a + b = b + a
$$
#### Associative property of Addition
$$
	(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)
$$
#### Communitive Property of Multiplication
$$
	a × b = b × a
$$
#### Associative Property of multiplication
$$
	(a × b) × c = a × (b × c)
$$
### Exponents
#### Product of Powers
$$
	a^m × a^n = a^{m+n}
$$
#### Quotient of Powers
$$
	a^m ÷ a^n = a^{m-n}
$$
#### Power of a  Power
$$
	(a^m)^n = a^{m\times n}
$$
#### Zero Exponent
$$
	a^0 = 1 (a ≠ 0)
$$
#### Negative Exponent
$$
	a^{-n} = 1/a^n
$$

### Quadratics
#### Quadratic Equation
$$
	x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{ b^2-(4ac) }}{2a}
$$
#### Discriminant
$$
	D = \frac{b^2-4ac}{2}
$$
#### Difference of Squares
$$
	a^2-b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)
$$
#### Perfect Square Trinomial
$$
a^2 + 2ab + b^2 = (a+b)^2
$$
### Linear Equations
#### Slope Formula
$$
	m = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)
$$
#### Slope Intercept Form
$$
	y = mx + b
$$
#### Point Slope Form
$$
	y - y₁ = m(x - x₁)
$$
#### Standard Form
$$
	Ax + By = C
$$
