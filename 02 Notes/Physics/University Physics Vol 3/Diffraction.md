# Diffraction

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 3]] › Unit 1 — Optics › Chapter 4
> ← [[Interference]] · Next: [[Relativity]] →

**Diffraction** is waves bending around edges and openings. It shapes single-slit patterns, sharpens grating spectra, and sets the limit on how finely instruments can resolve detail.

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> - **Single-slit minima:** $\displaystyle a\sin\theta = m\lambda,\quad m = \pm1, \pm2, \ldots$
> - **Half phase angle:** $\displaystyle \beta = \frac{\phi}{2} = \frac{\pi a\sin\theta}{\lambda}$
> - **Intensity:** $\displaystyle I = I_0\left(\frac{\sin\beta}{\beta}\right)^2$
> - **Rayleigh criterion:** $\displaystyle \theta = 1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}$
> - **Bragg equation:** $\displaystyle m\lambda = 2d\sin\theta,\quad m = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

---

## Single-Slit Diffraction
- A single slit makes a wide central maximum with narrower, dimmer maxima on each side.

> [!equation]
> - **Single-slit minima:** $\displaystyle a\sin\theta = m\lambda,\quad m = \pm1, \pm2, \ldots$

## Intensity in Single-Slit Diffraction
- Phasors give the intensity pattern; $a$ is slit width, $\theta$ the angle from the central peak.

> [!equation]
> - **Half phase angle:** $\displaystyle \beta = \frac{\phi}{2} = \frac{\pi a\sin\theta}{\lambda}$
> - **Intensity:** $\displaystyle I = I_0\left(\frac{\sin\beta}{\beta}\right)^2$

## Double-Slit Diffraction
- With finite-width slits, interference and diffraction combine.
- **Missing orders** occur where an interference maximum lands on a diffraction minimum.

> [!note] Connection
> See [[Interference]].

## Diffraction Gratings
- A **grating** has many evenly spaced slits, giving a sharper pattern than a double slit with the same bright-fringe condition $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$.

> [!note] Connection
> The condition is the same as for constructive interference in [[Interference]].

## Circular Apertures and Resolution
- Diffraction limits resolution.
- **Rayleigh criterion:** two images are just resolved when one pattern's center sits on the other's first minimum.

> [!equation]
> - **Rayleigh criterion:** $\displaystyle \theta = 1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}$

> [!note] Connection
> This limits microscopes and telescopes — [[Geometric Optics and Image Formation]].

## X-Ray Diffraction
- X-rays have short wavelengths and interfere when they scatter off very small structures such as crystal planes.

> [!equation]
> - **Bragg equation:** $\displaystyle m\lambda = 2d\sin\theta,\quad m = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

> [!note] Connection
> X-ray production is in [[Atomic Structure]].

## Holography
- Holography records 3D images using interference; lasers (monochromatic, coherent) make sharp holograms practical.

> [!note] Connection
> Lasers are explained in [[Atomic Structure]].
