# Linear Momentum and Collisions

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 1]] › Unit 1 — Mechanics › Chapter 9
> ← [[Potential Energy and Conservation of Energy]] · Next: [[Fixed-Axis Rotation]] →

**Linear Momentum and Collisions** introduces momentum, a quantity conserved in closed systems — the key tool for collisions and explosions.

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> - **Momentum:** $\displaystyle \vec{p} = m\vec{v}$
> - **Impulse:** $\displaystyle \vec{J} = \int_{t_i}^{t_f} \vec{F}(t)\,dt = \vec{F}_{\text{ave}}\,\Delta t$
> - **Impulse-momentum theorem:** $\displaystyle \vec{J} = \Delta\vec{p}$
> - **Newton's second law (momentum form):** $\displaystyle \vec{F}(t) = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$
> - **Conservation of momentum:** $\displaystyle \vec{p}_1 + \vec{p}_2 = \text{constant}$
> - **Generalized conservation:** $\displaystyle \sum_{j=1}^{N} \vec{p}_j = \text{constant}$
> - **Conservation in 2D:** $\displaystyle p_{f,x} = p_{1,i,x} + p_{2,i,x},\quad p_{f,y} = p_{1,i,y} + p_{2,i,y}$
> - **Center of mass (particles):** $\displaystyle \vec{r}_{\text{CM}} = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{j=1}^{N} m_j\vec{r}_j$
> - **Center of mass (continuous):** $\displaystyle \vec{r}_{\text{CM}} = \frac{1}{M}\int \vec{r}\,dm$
> - **Newton's second law for an extended object:** $\displaystyle \vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}_{\text{CM}}}{dt}$
> - **Rocket equation:** $\displaystyle \Delta v = u\ln\left(\frac{m_i}{m}\right)$

## Linear Momentum
- Momentum combines mass and velocity; SI unit kg·m/s.

> [!equation]
> - **Momentum:** $\displaystyle \vec{p} = m\vec{v}$

## Impulse and Collisions
- A force acting over time gives an **impulse**, which equals the change in momentum.
- Newton's second law can be written as net force = rate of change of momentum.

> [!equation]
> - **Impulse:** $\displaystyle \vec{J} = \int_{t_i}^{t_f} \vec{F}(t)\,dt = \vec{F}_{\text{ave}}\,\Delta t$
> - **Impulse-momentum theorem:** $\displaystyle \vec{J} = \Delta\vec{p}$
> - **Newton's second law (momentum form):** $\displaystyle \vec{F}(t) = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$

> [!note] Connection
> See [[Newton's Laws of Motion]] for $\vec F = m\vec a$.

## Conservation of Linear Momentum
- Total momentum of a **closed** system (constant mass, zero net external force) stays constant.

> [!equation]
> - **Conservation of momentum:** $\displaystyle \vec{p}_1 + \vec{p}_2 = \text{constant}$
> - **Generalized conservation:** $\displaystyle \sum_{j=1}^{N} \vec{p}_j = \text{constant}$

> [!note] Connection
> The rotational counterpart is conservation of angular momentum — [[Angular Momentum]].

## Types of Collisions
- **Elastic** collisions conserve kinetic energy; **inelastic** ones don't.
- Momentum is conserved in both.

> [!note] Connection
> Kinetic energy is defined in [[Work and Kinetic Energy]].

## Collisions in Multiple Dimensions
- Split motion into perpendicular components; momentum is conserved along each axis independently.

> [!equation]
> - **Conservation in 2D:** $\displaystyle p_{f,x} = p_{1,i,x} + p_{2,i,x},\quad p_{f,y} = p_{1,i,y} + p_{2,i,y}$

## Center of Mass
- The **center of mass** is the average location of an object's mass.
- It moves as Newton's second law dictates from the net external force; internal forces can't change total momentum.

> [!equation]
> - **Center of mass (particles):** $\displaystyle \vec{r}_{\text{CM}} = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{j=1}^{N} m_j\vec{r}_j$
> - **Center of mass (continuous):** $\displaystyle \vec{r}_{\text{CM}} = \frac{1}{M}\int \vec{r}\,dm$
> - **Newton's second law for an extended object:** $\displaystyle \vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}_{\text{CM}}}{dt}$

## Rocket Propulsion
- A rocket conserves momentum while its mass changes, ejecting fuel to produce thrust.

> [!equation]
> - **Rocket equation:** $\displaystyle \Delta v = u\ln\left(\frac{m_i}{m}\right)$
