## Electric Charge
$$
q_{e} = -1.6 \times 10^{-19} c
$$
$$
	m_{e} = 9.1 \times 10^{-31}kg
$$
## Proton
$$
	q_{p} = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}c
$$
$$
	m_{p} = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}kg
$$

Electric charge is conserved. It can never be less than $$q_e$$
Total charge will be equal to the number of electrons times q_e
$$
	Q_{total} = Nq_{e}
$$
$$
	Q_{net} = Nq_{p}
$$

Charged can be both moving or static
Identical charged repel 
Opposite charged attract 
Electrons are the only charges that move in an object
electric potential

$$
	F =  \frac{1}{4\pi \epsilon_{0}} \frac{{q_{1}q_{2}}}{r^2}
$$
Epsilon means electric permitivity of vacum or air

$$
	epsilon_{0} = 8.85 \times 10^{-12} \frac{c}{Nm^2}
$$
$$
	k = 9\times 10^{9} \frac{Nm^2}{c^2}
$$
[[Coulombs Law]]
$$
	k = \frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2}= Coulombs \space Law
$$
