# Atomic Structure

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 3]] › Unit 2 — Modern Physics › Chapter 8
> ← [[Quantum Mechanics]] · Next: [[Condensed Matter Physics]] →

**Atomic Structure** applies [[Quantum Mechanics]] to atoms: quantum numbers, spin, the exclusion principle, and how they explain the periodic table, spectra, X-rays and lasers.

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> - **Orbital angular momentum:** $\displaystyle L = \sqrt{l(l+1)}\,\hbar$
> - **z-component:** $\displaystyle L_z = m\hbar$
> - **Radial probability density:** $\displaystyle P(r)\,dr = |\psi_{n00}|^2\,4\pi r^2\,dr$
> - **Orbital magnetic dipole moment:** $\displaystyle \vec{\mu} = -\left(\frac{e}{2m_e}\right)\vec{L}$
> - **Energy in a magnetic field:** $\displaystyle U(\theta) = -\mu_z B = m\,\mu_B B$
> - **Spin angular momentum:** $\displaystyle S = \sqrt{s(s+1)}\,\hbar$
> - **z-component of spin:** $\displaystyle S_z = m_s\hbar$
> - **Spin magnetic moment:** $\displaystyle \vec{\mu}_s = \left(\frac{e}{m_e}\right)\vec{S}$
> - **Electrons per subshell:** $\displaystyle N = 4l + 2$
> - **Selection rule:** $\displaystyle \Delta l = \pm 1$
> - **Moseley's law:** $\displaystyle (Z - 1) = \text{constant}\,\sqrt{f}$

## The Hydrogen Atom
- The electron's state is set by quantum numbers $n$, $l$ and $m$.
- Unlike the Bohr model, the Schrödinger model is probabilistic.

> [!equation]
> - **Orbital angular momentum:** $\displaystyle L = \sqrt{l(l+1)}\,\hbar$
> - **z-component:** $\displaystyle L_z = m\hbar$
> - **Radial probability density:** $\displaystyle P(r)\,dr = |\psi_{n00}|^2\,4\pi r^2\,dr$

> [!note] Connection
> Compare the Bohr model in [[Photons and Matter Waves]].

## Orbital Magnetic Dipole Moment of the Electron
- The orbiting electron acts like a current loop, so hydrogen has magnetic properties.
- An external field splits energy levels because the orbital moment interacts with it.

> [!equation]
> - **Orbital magnetic dipole moment:** $\displaystyle \vec{\mu} = -\left(\frac{e}{2m_e}\right)\vec{L}$
> - **Energy in a magnetic field:** $\displaystyle U(\theta) = -\mu_z B = m\,\mu_B B$

> [!note] Connection
> A current loop's magnetic moment and its energy in a field are in [[Magnetic Forces and Fields]].

## Electron Spin
- The electron's state needs five quantum numbers; spin $s = \tfrac12$ with $m_s = \pm\tfrac12$ (up or down).
- Magnetic interactions inside the atom explain fine and hyperfine structure.

> [!equation]
> - **Spin angular momentum:** $\displaystyle S = \sqrt{s(s+1)}\,\hbar$
> - **z-component of spin:** $\displaystyle S_z = m_s\hbar$
> - **Spin magnetic moment:** $\displaystyle \vec{\mu}_s = \left(\frac{e}{m_e}\right)\vec{S}$

## The Exclusion Principle and the Periodic Table
- **Pauli exclusion principle:** no two electrons in an atom share all quantum numbers.
- Energy, orbital angular momentum and spin explain the periodic table's structure; an **electron configuration** lists filled shells and subshells.

> [!equation]
> - **Electrons per subshell:** $\displaystyle N = 4l + 2$

## Atomic Spectra and X-rays
- Atoms absorb and emit radiation by jumping between energy levels.
- **Fluorescence:** a high-energy UV photon excites an electron several levels up.
- **X-rays** are emitted when an outer electron fills an inner-shell vacancy; their frequency depends on $Z$.

> [!equation]
> - **Selection rule:** $\displaystyle \Delta l = \pm 1$
> - **Moseley's law:** $\displaystyle (Z - 1) = \text{constant}\,\sqrt{f}$

> [!note] Connection
> X-rays are used in [[Diffraction]] (Bragg's law).

## Lasers
- Laser light is **coherent** — monochromatic and phase-linked.
- Produced by **population inversion** and stimulated de-excitation; used in CD and Blu-ray players.

> [!note] Connection
> Lasers make holography practical — [[Diffraction]].
