## Introduction to Particle Physics
- Four forces, strongest to weakest: strong nuclear, electromagnetic, weak nuclear, gravitational.
- Quarks feel the strong force; leptons don't. Both feel the other three.
- **Fermions** (half-integer spin) obey the exclusion principle; **bosons** (integer spin) don't and carry the forces.
- Quarks and leptons come in three families; every particle has an **antiparticle** with opposite charge.

> [!note] Connection
> The exclusion principle and spin are in [[Atomic Structure]].

## Particle Conservation Laws
- Conservation laws decide which reactions and decays can happen.
- Baryon number and the three lepton numbers are always conserved; strangeness only in strong and electromagnetic interactions.

## Quarks
- Six quarks — up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom — are fermions with fractional charge.
- **Baryons** are three quarks; **mesons** are a quark–antiquark pair. Quarks can't exist alone.

## Particle Accelerators and Detectors
- Accelerators: linear accelerators, cyclotrons, synchrotrons, colliding beams.
- Magnetic fields bend charged particles in circles so their momentum can be measured; calorimeters absorb particles to measure energy.

> [!equation]
> - **Momentum in a cyclotron:** $\displaystyle p = 0.3Br$
> - **Collider center-of-mass energy:** $\displaystyle W^2 = 2\left[E_1E_2 + (p_1c)(p_2c)\right] + (m_1c^2)^2 + (m_2c^2)^2$

> [!note] Connection
> Circular motion of a charge in a magnetic field ($r = mv/qB$) is in [[Magnetic Forces and Fields]].

## The Standard Model
- Describes strong, electromagnetic and weak interactions; forces are carried by exchanged bosons.
- Electromagnetism is long-range; strong and weak forces are short-range. **Feynman diagrams** picture interactions.
- Grand unified theories aim to merge all forces into one.

> [!equation]
> - **Virtual-particle exchange time:** $\displaystyle \Delta t = \frac{h}{E}$

## The Big Bang
- The universe is expanding; distant galaxies recede at speeds proportional to distance (about 70 km/s/Mpc), measured by Doppler shift.
- Current models put the Big Bang about 13.7 billion years ago.

> [!equation]
> - **Hubble's law:** $\displaystyle v = H_0 d$

> [!note] Connection
> Recession speeds come from the Doppler effect for light — [[Relativity]].

## Evolution of the Early Universe
- The early universe was hot and dense; the universe is isotropic and expanding.
- **Cosmic background radiation** supports the Big Bang.
- Most of the universe's mass and energy is not well understood.

> [!equation]
> - **Cosmological space-time metric:** $\displaystyle ds^2 = c^2\,dt^2 - a(t)^2\,d\Sigma^2$
