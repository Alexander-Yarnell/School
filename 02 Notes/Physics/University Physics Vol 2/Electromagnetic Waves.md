# Electromagnetic Waves

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 2]] › Unit 2 — Electricity and Magnetism › Chapter 16
> ← [[Alternating-Current Circuits]] · Next: [[The Nature of Light]] (Vol 3) →

**Electromagnetic Waves** closes the volume. Maxwell's equations unify electricity and magnetism and predict waves of electric and magnetic fields traveling at the speed of light — showing that **light is an electromagnetic wave**.

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> $$\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I + \mu_0\varepsilon_0\frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$$
> $$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}} \approx 3.00\times10^{8}\ \text{m/s}$$
> $$E = cB$$
> $$\vec{S} = \frac{1}{\mu_0}\vec{E}\times\vec{B} \qquad I = \tfrac{1}{2}c\varepsilon_0 E_0^2$$
> $$p_{\text{rad}} = \frac{I}{c}\ (\text{absorbed}) \qquad p_{\text{rad}} = \frac{2I}{c}\ (\text{reflected})$$
> $$c = f\lambda$$

## Maxwell's Equations
Four laws unify electricity and magnetism:

| Law | Where it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Gauss's law for $\vec{E}$ | [[Gauss's Law]] |
| Gauss's law for $\vec{B}$ | (named in this chapter) |
| Faraday's law | [[Electromagnetic Induction]] |
| Ampère-Maxwell law | [[Sources of Magnetic Fields]] (Ampère's law) + displacement current |

Maxwell's **displacement current** means a changing electric field creates a magnetic field:

> [!equation]
> $$\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I + \mu_0\varepsilon_0\frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$$

> [!note] Connection
> Together with Faraday's law (a changing $\vec{B}$ creates $\vec{E}$), this is how changing fields create each other.

## Plane Electromagnetic Waves
In a plane wave, $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$ oscillate perpendicular to each other and to the direction of travel:

> [!equation]
> $$E = cB$$
> $$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}} \approx 3.00\times10^{8}\ \text{m/s}$$

> [!note] Connection
> $\varepsilon_0$ is the constant from [[Gauss's Law]] and Coulomb's law; $\mu_0$ is the constant from the Biot-Savart and Ampère's laws in [[Sources of Magnetic Fields]].

## Energy and Intensity
The **Poynting vector** gives the direction and rate of energy flow:

> [!equation]
> $$\vec{S} = \frac{1}{\mu_0}\vec{E}\times\vec{B} \qquad I = \tfrac{1}{2}c\varepsilon_0 E_0^2$$

## Momentum and Radiation Pressure
Light pushes on surfaces. The radiation pressure is

> [!equation]
> $$p_{\text{rad}} = \frac{I}{c}\ (\text{absorbed}) \qquad p_{\text{rad}} = \frac{2I}{c}\ (\text{reflected})$$

This is the idea behind **solar sails**.

## The Electromagnetic Spectrum
Radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays are all the same physics at different wavelengths and frequencies:

> [!equation]
> $$c = f\lambda$$
