# Waves

> [!info] [[University Physics Vol 1]] › Unit 2 — Waves and Acoustics › Chapter 16
> ← [[Oscillations]] · Next: [[Sound]] →

**Waves** are disturbances that travel and carry energy. This chapter treats mechanical waves on strings and in media, interference, and standing waves.

## Key Equations

> [!equation]
> - **Wave speed:** $\displaystyle v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = \lambda f$
> - **Wave function:** $\displaystyle y(x,t) = A\sin(kx \mp \omega t + \phi)$
> - **Wave number:** $\displaystyle k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$
> - **Wave speed:** $\displaystyle v = \frac{\omega}{k}$
> - **Linear wave equation:** $\displaystyle \frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial x^2} = \frac{1}{v_w^2}\frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial t^2}$
> - **Speed on a string:** $\displaystyle v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}}$
> - **Speed of a compression wave in a fluid:** $\displaystyle v = \sqrt{\frac{B}{\rho}}$
> - **Average power on a string:** $\displaystyle P_{\text{ave}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\mu A^2\omega^2 v$
> - **Intensity:** $\displaystyle I = \frac{P}{A}$
> - **Intensity of a spherical wave:** $\displaystyle I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$
> - **Superposition of two phase-shifted waves:** $\displaystyle y_R(x,t) = \left[2A\cos\left(\frac{\phi}{2}\right)\right]\sin\left(kx - \omega t + \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$
> - **Standing wave:** $\displaystyle y(x,t) = [2A\sin(kx)]\cos(\omega t)$
> - **Wavelengths (both ends fixed):** $\displaystyle \lambda_n = \frac{2}{n}L$
> - **Frequencies (both ends fixed):** $\displaystyle f_n = n\frac{v}{2L} = nf_1$

## Traveling Waves
- **Wavelength** is the distance between identical points on the wave.
- **Mechanical** waves need a medium; **electromagnetic** waves don't; **matter** waves belong to quantum mechanics.
- **Transverse** waves disturb perpendicular to travel; **longitudinal** waves parallel to it.

> [!equation]
> - **Wave speed:** $\displaystyle v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = \lambda f$

> [!note] Connection
> Electromagnetic waves are covered in [[Electromagnetic Waves]]; matter waves in [[Photons and Matter Waves]].

## Mathematics of Waves
- A sinusoidal wave is described by its wave number $k$, angular frequency $\omega$ and phase shift $\phi$.
- Replace $x$ with $x \mp vt$ for a wave moving in the $\pm x$ direction.

> [!equation]
> - **Wave function:** $\displaystyle y(x,t) = A\sin(kx \mp \omega t + \phi)$
> - **Wave number:** $\displaystyle k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$
> - **Wave speed:** $\displaystyle v = \frac{\omega}{k}$
> - **Linear wave equation:** $\displaystyle \frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial x^2} = \frac{1}{v_w^2}\frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial t^2}$

## Wave Speed on a Stretched String
- Speed depends on the square root of (elastic property ÷ inertial property).
- On a string: tension and linear mass density. In a fluid: bulk modulus and density.
- Sound in air at 20 °C travels about 343 m/s.

> [!equation]
> - **Speed on a string:** $\displaystyle v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}}$
> - **Speed of a compression wave in a fluid:** $\displaystyle v = \sqrt{\frac{B}{\rho}}$

> [!note] Connection
> Bulk modulus is from [[Static Equilibrium and Elasticity]].

## Energy and Power of a Wave
- Energy and power go as amplitude squared and angular frequency squared.
- Intensity is power per area; for a spherical wave it falls as $1/r^2$.

> [!equation]
> - **Average power on a string:** $\displaystyle P_{\text{ave}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\mu A^2\omega^2 v$
> - **Intensity:** $\displaystyle I = \frac{P}{A}$
> - **Intensity of a spherical wave:** $\displaystyle I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$

## Interference of Waves
- **Superposition**: waves at the same place add.
- In phase → **constructive** interference; 180° out of phase → **destructive**.

> [!equation]
> - **Superposition of two phase-shifted waves:** $\displaystyle y_R(x,t) = \left[2A\cos\left(\frac{\phi}{2}\right)\right]\sin\left(kx - \omega t + \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$

## Standing Waves and Resonance
- A **standing wave** comes from two waves superposing; it doesn't travel.
- **Nodes** don't move; **antinodes** have maximum amplitude.
- The lowest normal mode is the **fundamental**; higher ones are **overtones**.

> [!equation]
> - **Standing wave:** $\displaystyle y(x,t) = [2A\sin(kx)]\cos(\omega t)$
> - **Wavelengths (both ends fixed):** $\displaystyle \lambda_n = \frac{2}{n}L$
> - **Frequencies (both ends fixed):** $\displaystyle f_n = n\frac{v}{2L} = nf_1$

> [!note] Connection
> The same patterns appear in air columns — [[Sound]].
